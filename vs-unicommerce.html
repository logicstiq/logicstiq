Comparison

LogicstIQ vs Unicommerce: different layers, not rivals

We get asked this often enough to answer it properly, including the parts where they win. The short version: Unicommerce is built to execute your orders. LogicstIQ is built to decide what you should buy and what it will actually earn. Those are different jobs, and plenty of sellers need both.

Where each one sits in the stack

Most confusion about this comparison disappears once the layers are drawn out.

The e-commerce operations stack
Decide What to buy, how much, when, at what true margin. LogicstIQ
Execute Process the order, allocate stock, pick, pack, invoice. Uniware and platforms like it
Deliver Courier allocation, NDR handling, tracking, returns processing. Shipway and similar
Engage WhatsApp, SMS, retention campaigns. Convertway and similar

Unicommerce has built across the bottom three layers, and does so at real scale, with thousands of clients and a very large share of Indian dropship shipment volume. We work in the top layer only, and we intend to keep it that way.

Choosing honestly

Start with us if…

Your fulfilment already works but you're guessing at purchase quantities, planning in Excel, unsure which SKUs actually make money, or you've been quoted enterprise pricing that doesn't match your order volume yet.

Start with them if…

Your bottleneck is fulfilment, not planning: several warehouses, item-level barcoding, handheld picking, courier labels at volume, or a procurement process that requires ISO certification and analyst coverage before it can sign.

Run both if…

You're past roughly 5,000 orders a month. Export your sales history, plan here, and raise the resulting purchase orders in the platform that actually moves your stock. That's the setup we designed for.

Questions

Is LogicstIQ a Unicommerce alternative?

Not a direct one, and we'd rather not be sold as one. They run your operations; we tell you what to buy and what it earns. If someone pitches a planning tool as a replacement for an execution platform, be sceptical, including of us.

Can I use both together?

Yes, and above roughly 5,000 orders a month that's usually the right answer. Export sales history from your execution platform, plan in LogicstIQ, and raise the resulting POs back where your stock actually moves.

Why should I trust a comparison written by you?

You shouldn't take it on faith, which is why the table above lists nine rows where they win and names the specific reasons. Everything is drawn from their public pages and ours, and we'll correct anything you can show is wrong.

What happens when you build OMS and WMS?

They'll be planning-first and deliberately narrow: cost, capacity and contribution, not pick paths and barcode scanning. We're not trying to become an execution platform. Our OMS approach · our WMS approach.
