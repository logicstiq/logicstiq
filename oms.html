Order & fulfilment

Every channel in one view, and what each order actually earned

Most order management tools tell you an order arrived. Ours is being built to tell you what it contributed after commission, fulfilment fees, GST and the odds of it coming back. Same order list, a column nobody else shows.

Use the planner today → Join the OMS waitlist

Status: in active development. The economics engine behind it is live today inside the AI Demand Planner.

What it is being built to do

Deliberately narrow. We are not rebuilding a warehouse execution system. We are adding the decision layer that order lists normally lack.

One order list, every channel

Marketplace, D2C and quick-commerce orders normalised into a single view with consistent SKU identity, so a Flipkart FSN and an Amazon ASIN for the same product stop being two different products.

Contribution per order, not just value

Order value is easy. Order contribution, after that channel's commission, fulfilment and closing fees, net GST and its RTO profile, is the number that tells you whether the day was good.

Exceptions worth your attention

Not a wall of notifications. Orders flagged because they are unprofitable at that discount, at risk of breaching a promise date, or concentrated in a pincode with a poor delivery record.

How it will fit with what you already run

  1. It reads, it doesn't take over

    Your marketplace panels and any WMS you use stay the source of truth for fulfilment. We read order data through official APIs and file exports, and write back nothing.

  2. SKU identity gets resolved first

    The same normaliser that powers the demand planner maps FSN, ASIN, FNSKU and your internal SKU codes onto one product identity. Without that step, per-order economics are meaningless.

  3. Channel economics are applied per order

    Commission, fees, net GST and the channel RTO profile come from the same models the planner uses, so the number on an order agrees with the number in your forecast. See the calculation →

  4. Then it feeds the plan

    Actual orders become the history the forecast learns from, and the forecast-versus-actual accuracy badge gets sharper every cycle. The loop closing is the point of building this at all.

Where an OMS like ours is the wrong tool. If you need order routing across multiple fulfilment centres, pick-pack workflows on handheld scanners, or courier label generation at scale, a dedicated execution platform will serve you far better and we will happily say so. Where the line sits →
