Returns & recovery

Returns are not a customer service problem. They are your margin.

Most returns tools focus on processing the return smoothly. Useful, but it does not tell you that a bestseller is losing money, or which channel is responsible. We price RTO into every SKU first, then show you where the recoverable rupees are.

See what RTO costs you → How the maths works

Status: RTO economics and profit-leak detection are live today. Return-processing workflows are in development.

What you can see today

This is the one area where we would argue we are already ahead of platforms many times our size.

True contribution, RTO included

Forward shipping, the return leg, handling, and the share of returned units that come back unsellable, all subtracted per SKU, per channel. A SKU showing 25% margin often contributes closer to 10%.

Profit leaks, ranked

High-revenue SKUs whose contribution is near zero or negative once returns are priced in. They are almost always bestsellers, which is exactly why nobody notices them.

What a fix is worth

Before you rewrite a size chart or restrict COD on a pincode, see the rupee value of a two-point RTO reduction on that SKU. It makes the argument for the work.

Where returns show up across the platform

  1. In the forecast

    Demand net of returns, so a reorder quantity is not inflated by units that were never really sold. Forecasting gross of RTO is how sellers end up overstocked on their worst SKUs.

  2. In the buy plan

    Budget is allocated by protected contribution per rupee, which naturally starves high-RTO SKUs of working capital without you having to police them manually.

  3. In carrier selection

    The TMS rate shop is RTO-aware: a cheaper forward rate with a worse return record can cost more per delivered order. Cost per delivered order is the comparison that matters.

  4. In processing (in development)

    Return receipt, disposition decisions and restock-versus-write-off recommendations are being built. For Amazon FBA, disposition is already live. FBA disposition →

What we don't do yet. We are not a customer-facing returns portal. No branded return pages, no automated refunds, no exchange flows. If that is your bottleneck, a dedicated post-purchase tool is the right buy. We handle the money side of returns, not the conversation side.
