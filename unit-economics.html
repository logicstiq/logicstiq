Unit economics

Your margin report is lying to you. RTO is the reason.

Every seller knows their listed margin. Almost none know their contribution per unit after marketplace commission, fulfilment and closing fees, GST, and the real cost of returns. LogicstIQ computes it per SKU, per channel, and tells you which of your bestsellers are quietly funded by you.

Why this matters more than it sounds. Restock decisions are made on margin. If the margin figure is wrong by 60%, every reorder quantity, every EOQ, every budget allocation built on it is wrong too, and the error compounds every cycle. Figures above are illustrative; the engine uses your channel rates and your measured RTO.

What the engine subtracts, and where it gets the numbers

  1. Marketplace commission, per channel and category

    Amazon.in, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Nykaa, Ajio and quick-commerce rates are pre-loaded as defaults. Override any of them with your actual negotiated rate.

  2. Fulfilment, closing and weight-slab fees

    Pick-pack, weight handling and closing fees, including FBA fee structures for Amazon and the equivalents on Flipkart Smart and Myntra PPMP.

  3. GST, netted correctly

    Output GST against input credit on cost and freight, so the number reflects what you actually remit rather than the headline rate.

  4. RTO, the part nobody models

    Your measured return rate per channel, applied to forward shipping, the return leg, handling, and the share of returned units that come back unsellable. This is where most of the margin goes.

  5. Cost per delivered order from your carriers

    If you use the TMS rate shop, real weight-slab, zone and COD costs feed straight into this calculation instead of an average.

  6. Then it flags the leaks

    SKUs with strong revenue and healthy listed margin whose true contribution is near zero or negative. Usually your bestsellers, which is exactly why they go unnoticed.

What you do with the answer

The buy plan ranks SKUs by protected contribution per rupee of working capital, so a limited budget goes to the stock that actually earns. Partial fills included.

Questions

Why does RTO change my margin so much?

An RTO unit costs you forward shipping, the return leg, handling, and often the product itself if it comes back damaged or unsellable — while earning zero revenue. At a 24% return rate, every four units sold fund one unit of pure loss. That is why a SKU showing 25% margin on paper can contribute close to nothing.

Do I need to know my exact fee structure?

No. Channel and category defaults are pre-loaded for every major Indian marketplace. Override any of them with your negotiated rates and the whole calculation updates. Accuracy improves as you replace defaults with your real numbers.

What counts as a profit leak?

A SKU with healthy revenue and a healthy listed margin whose true contribution is near zero or negative once fees and RTO are subtracted. They are almost always high-volume lines, because volume is what makes the return cost material.

Can I trust these numbers for accounting?

No, and you shouldn't. These are planning figures for purchasing decisions. Reconcile against your marketplace settlement reports and your books before filing anything. Full disclaimer.
