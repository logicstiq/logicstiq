The complete procure-to-pay paper trail: purchase requisitions, RFQs, supplier comparisons, purchase orders carrying marketplace identifiers (ASIN, FNSKU, FSN), landed-cost worksheets, inbound ASNs and carton manifests for FC deliveries, GRNs, and 2-way / 3-way matching. FBA Planner & Disposition Live

A full Amazon FBA demand-planning engine: automatic model selection with a probabilistic band, a send plan capped to your actual restock limits, FBA fee economics, long-term storage fee and aged-inventory risk with removal recommendations, stranded-inventory detection, and a Q4 storage forecast. Built on Amazon's official Selling Partner API.
