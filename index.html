Questions sellers ask

Is LogicstIQ completely free to use?

The 31 calculators are completely free forever, with no login and no signup. Our other tools, the AI Demand Planner, Paperwork Hub, Sourcing & Procurement, FBA Planner and TMS, are free as well, and only ask for a free account so we can save your work to the cloud. Why the sign-in exists →

What does "RTO-adjusted margin" actually mean?

Your listed margin ignores returns. We subtract marketplace commission, fulfilment and closing fees, GST, and the real cost of RTO per channel, so you see the rupees each SKU contributes, not the rupees you hoped it would. See the full calculation →

What file formats are supported?

Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, TSV and TXT up to 25 MB. Real Excel files are read natively. No converting to CSV first. Exports from Flipkart Seller Hub, Amazon.in Seller Central, Meesho, Myntra, Nykaa, Ajio, Shopify, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart and BigBasket all work, as does any other spreadsheet.

Does LogicstIQ store my inventory data?

Calculators store nothing; they run entirely in your browser. Signed-in tools save your files and forecasts to your private account, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Only computed summary numbers are ever sent to the AI, never your raw file. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from settings.

Does it work for Blinkit and Zepto suppliers?

Yes, and this is where we're strongest. The planner works per city, per dark store and per time of day, built for the short lead times and per-store volatility of 10-minute delivery, not adapted from a warehouse model. Quick commerce planning →

How accurate are the forecasts?

Accuracy depends on the length and quality of your sales history. Forecasts are computed statistically and returned with P50/P90/P95 confidence bands. The AI writes the explanation, never the numbers. Treat every output as a planning input and validate against your ERP or WMS before raising a PO.

Who is LogicstIQ for?

D2C brands, marketplace sellers on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and Myntra, manufacturers, distributors, C&F agents, 3PL companies, quick commerce suppliers, importers and exporters, and supply chain professionals.

Will you add more tools?

The TMS is in beta now. Order Management, Warehouse Management and Returns Management are in development. Saved forecasts, forecast-versus-actual accuracy and API access are part of the free account, not a paid tier. The full platform and roadmap →
