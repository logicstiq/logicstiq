Your own store Live

Shopify connects directly through an official OAuth app. We read orders, products and inventory through the Admin API, with HMAC verification on every callback. Amazon connects through the official Selling Partner API using a Login-with-Amazon refresh token to pull orders and FBA inventory. No scraping, no stored passwords, no browser automation. Both are the vendor-sanctioned route. WooCommerce and Magento work today via CSV export.

Shopify (direct OAuth) Amazon SP-API (direct) WooCommerce (via export) Magento (via export)

Carriers & shipping Beta

The TMS rate shop compares carriers on weight slab, zone and COD handling, and returns cost per delivered order, which is the number that matters once RTO is priced in. Live serviceability and rate lookups run through a Shiprocket account you own, which reaches Delhivery, Blue Dart, Xpressbees, DTDC, Ekart and Ecom Express through one connection. Read-only: nothing is ever booked or created on your behalf. Without credentials the TMS falls back to built-in benchmark rates, so it never breaks.
