Questions

Is LogicstIQ completely free to use?

The 31 calculators are completely free forever, with no login and no signup. Our other tools, the AI Demand Planner, Paperwork Hub, Sourcing & Procurement, FBA Planner and TMS, are free as well, and only ask for a free account so we can save your work to the cloud.

Then why do I have to sign in at all?

Because saved work has to belong to somebody. Without an account there is no way to keep your uploads separate from anyone else's, no way to show you last month's forecast next to what actually happened, and no way for you to delete it later. The account is what makes the data private. It is not a gate on the features.

Is anything held back until I pay?

Nothing is locked. If a tool is listed on this site and it is live, a free account reaches all of it. Our terms commit to this: the 31 calculators need no account and will remain free with no login, and the signed-in tools are free as well. Should we ever offer an optional paid add-on, it will sit alongside the free tools rather than replace them, it will be published on this page before it takes effect, and it will not withdraw access to anything you are already using. Read the terms.

Does LogicstIQ store my inventory data?

Calculators store nothing, they run entirely in your browser. For the signed-in tools we save your uploaded files and forecasts to your private account so you can return to them, stored securely and encrypted. We never sell or share your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time from your account settings. Only computed summary numbers are ever sent to the AI, never your raw file.

Is my data used to train an AI model?

No. Gemini AI receives only computed summary numbers, never your raw inventory file or personal data. Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers or third parties for commercial use.

What file formats can I upload?

Excel (.xlsx and .xls), CSV, TSV and TXT files up to 25MB. Real Excel files are read natively, so there is no converting to CSV first.

Can I use the calculators without an account forever?

Yes. They run in your browser and cost us nothing to serve, so there is no reason to ever put them behind a login.
